Clinical follow-up guide

Next steps by screening likelihood

Use the probability range from the screening result to identify the appropriate follow-up. These recommendations support, but do not replace, clinical judgment.

0–25%Low risk

FH is unlikely based on the information provided.

Recommended actions

  • Continue routine cholesterol screening.
  • Maintain heart-healthy habits.
  • Recheck cholesterol levels during regular medical visits.
25–50%Moderate risk

Some features suggest possible FH.

Recommended actions

  • Discuss results with a healthcare provider.
  • Get a full lipid panel, especially LDL cholesterol.
  • Review family history of high cholesterol or early heart disease.
50–75%High risk

FH is more likely.

Recommended actions

  • Schedule a clinical evaluation.
  • Consider genetic testing for FH-related genes such as LDLR, APOB, and PCSK9.
  • Screen close relatives because FH can run in families.
75–100%Very high risk

Strong likelihood of FH.

Recommended actions

  • Schedule a clinical evaluation.
  • Confirm with cholesterol measurements and/or genetic testing.
  • Begin appropriate management if confirmed.
